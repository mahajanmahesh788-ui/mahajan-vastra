About Our Sacred Craft

Devoting our skills to divine service

Preserving Sacred Traditions

At Mahajan God Idols Dresses Stitching (Vastra), we understand the profound significance of adorning your beloved deities with the finest sacred garments. Our devotion to this ancient craft has been passed down through generations, ensuring each stitch carries the blessing of tradition.

Located in the heart of Pathankot, Punjab, our women-owned business specializes in creating exquisite vastra for god idols, with particular expertise in Shree Krishna and Radha Rani dresses. Every piece we create is imbued with spiritual intention and meticulous attention to detail.

Our Sacred Craft Process

Each vastra is created with devotion, following traditional methods passed down through generations. From fabric selection to final stitching, every step honors the divine purpose of the garment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to your spiritual questions

For which deities do you stitch vastra?

We lovingly stitch sacred vastra for all Hindu deities. Every vastra is prepared with purity, devotion, and respect, keeping in mind traditional values and proper fitting.

हम सभी देवी-देवताओं के लिए पवित्र वस्त्र सिलाई करते हैं। प्रत्येक वस्त्र शुद्धता, श्रद्धा और पारंपरिक मर्यादा को ध्यान में रखकर तैयार किया जाता है।

Is this a business or a seva?

This is not just a business — it is seva. We work with a devotional mindset, focusing on cleanliness, simplicity, and grace rather than profit.

यह केवल व्यवसाय नहीं, बल्कि सेवा है। हमारा उद्देश्य लाभ नहीं, बल्कि श्रद्धा, स्वच्छता और भक्ति भाव से सेवा करना है।

Can I choose fabric, color, and design?

Yes, you are welcome to choose the fabric, color, and design as per your devotion and preference. We also guide you if you need simple and graceful suggestions.

जी हाँ, आप अपनी पसंद के अनुसार कपड़ा, रंग और डिज़ाइन चुन सकते हैं। आवश्यकता होने पर हम सरल और सुंदर सुझाव भी देते हैं।

How much time does it take to prepare the vastra?

Usually, vastra preparation takes a week depending on the design and seva queue. Festival times may take slightly longer, but we always inform you clearly.

सामान्यतः वस्त्र तैयार होने में कुछ दिन लगते हैं। त्योहारों के समय थोड़ा अधिक समय लग सकता है, जिसकी जानकारी पहले ही दे दी जाती है।
